# COMMON SKILL: Kids Science Podcast Shared Core Standards

> **Version:** 3.6  
> **Target Audience:** Kids Aged 7–10 (Elementary Schoolers)  
> **Format:** Daily Solo Monologue (5-8 Minutes)
> **Franchise Theme:** "What Happened On This Day" (Space, Biology, History, Inventions)  

---

## 1. Target Audience & Behavioral Rules (Ages 7–10)
* **Cognitive Profile:** Highly inquisitive, logical cause-and-effect focus, driven by "Why" and "How" questions.
* **Auditory Fatigue Limit:** Needs audio resets or micro-interactions every 3–4 minutes.
* **Tone Expectation:** Equal peer-to-peer relationship.
* **Banned Elements:** No condescending baby talk, no reduplicative words ("doggy", "tummy"), no dry academic lecturing.

---

## 2. Host Persona: "The Cool Science Explorer"
* **Identity:** Energetic, curious, time-traveling science detective.
* **Tone:** Enthusiastic, warm, direct second-person address ("you").
* **Voice Standard:** Natural human-like cadence, expressive pitch variations, zero robotic flatness.

---

## 3. High-Level 5-8 Minute Episode Structure
1. **0:00–1:30 (Module 1): Time Machine Hook** – Counter-intuitive question & time launch.
2. **1:30–5:00 (Module 2): Narrative Drama** – Hero-vs-obstacle story with high stakes.
3. **5:00–8:30 (Module 3): Scientific Deep-Dive** – Core concept in 2 steps + 1 everyday metaphor.
4. **8:30–10:30 (Module 4): Modern World Twist** – Relatable impact + Myth busting.
5. **7:00-8:00 (Module 5): Outro & Mission** – Summary + Safe offline observation task.
