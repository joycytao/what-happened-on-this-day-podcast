# Research references

Episode date: 2026-10-12
Chosen subject: A royal wedding plants the seed for Oktoberfest

## ref-1

Oktoberfest.de: The history of Oktoberfest (https://www.oktoberfest.de/en/magazine/tradition/the-history-of-oktoberfest) says the wedding of Ludwig of Bavaria and Princess Therese of Saxe-Hildburghausen took place on October 12, 1810. The official festivities lasted five days and ended on October 17 with a horse race on a meadow outside Munich. The source also says the meadow was named Theresens-Wiese in honor of the bride, which supports the episode's place-based tradition angle.

Source: Oktoberfest.de: The history of Oktoberfest
URL: https://www.oktoberfest.de/en/magazine/tradition/the-history-of-oktoberfest
Source type: official

## ref-2

Muenchen.de: The History of the Oktoberfest in Munich (https://www.muenchen.de/en/events/oktoberfest/history-oktoberfest-munich) identifies October 17, 1810 as the opening signal for the first Oktoberfest horse race, held for the wedding of Bavarian King Ludwig I and Princess Therese of Saxony-Hildburghausen. The source says the meadow was named Theresienwiese in honor of the bride and records later milestones such as the 1811 continuation and the 1818 arrival of rides.

Source: Muenchen.de: The History of the Oktoberfest in Munich
URL: https://www.muenchen.de/en/events/oktoberfest/history-oktoberfest-munich
Source type: official

## ref-3

Muenchen.de: Oktoberfest FAQ (https://www.muenchen.de/en/events/oktoberfest/visitor-service/faq) explains that the first Oktoberfest took place on October 17, 1810 with a large horse race for the wedding of Crown Prince Ludwig and Princess Therese. The FAQ also explains why Oktoberfest now starts in September: the festival moved earlier in 1872 to use milder weather while still ending near early October.

Source: Muenchen.de: Oktoberfest FAQ
URL: https://www.muenchen.de/en/events/oktoberfest/visitor-service/faq
Source type: official

## ref-4

Britannica: Oktoberfest (https://www.britannica.com/topic/Oktoberfest) describes Oktoberfest as an annual festival in Munich that originated with the October 12, 1810 marriage of Crown Prince Ludwig, later King Ludwig I, to Princess Therese von Sachsen-Hildburghausen. Britannica says the celebration concluded five days later with a horse race in an open area later called Theresienwiese.

Source: Britannica: Oktoberfest
URL: https://www.britannica.com/topic/Oktoberfest
Source type: reference
