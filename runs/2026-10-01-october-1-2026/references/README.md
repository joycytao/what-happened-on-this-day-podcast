# Research references

Episode date: 2026-10-01
Chosen subject: Ford Model T goes on sale

## ref-1

Ford Motor Company: The Model T (https://corporate.ford.com/articles/history/the-model-t.html) states that the Model T was introduced to the world in 1908 and that the vehicle was shipped to its first customer on October 1, 1908. The source explains Henry Ford's goal: a car that was affordable, simple to operate, and durable. It also says mass production let Ford sell the Model T for between $260 and $850 as production savings were passed to customers.

Source: Ford Motor Company: The Model T
URL: https://corporate.ford.com/articles/history/the-model-t.html
Source type: official

## ref-2

The Henry Ford: Ford's Model T: A Car for the Great Multitude (https://www.thehenryford.org/collections/explore/articles/fords-model-t) says Ford introduced the Model T on October 1, 1908. The source describes the car as inexpensive, efficient, and reliable, built to put automobile ownership within reach of more people. It notes the initial price of $850 and explains features that made the car practical for rough roads and owner maintenance.

Source: The Henry Ford: Ford's Model T: A Car for the Great Multitude
URL: https://www.thehenryford.org/collections/explore/articles/fords-model-t
Source type: museum

## ref-3

The Henry Ford artifact page for Advertisement for the 1909 Ford Model T, "The Ford Four Cylinder," October 1, 1908 (https://www.thehenryford.org/collections/explore/artifact/331428) documents an October 1, 1908 advertisement by Ford Motor Company. The artifact page says Ford emphasized technological features and low prices, and the inscription lists a five-passenger touring car price of $820 f.o.b. Detroit. This gives writers a concrete period detail for how Ford marketed the car.

Source: The Henry Ford: Advertisement for the 1909 Ford Model T, October 1, 1908
URL: https://www.thehenryford.org/collections/explore/artifact/331428
Source type: museum
